Working title: **The Jet That Couldn't Count** (Gimli Glider, Air Canada Flight 143, 23 July 1983)
Format: vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, target 60-68 s, English voice, one steady deadpan tone, no captions.

## Script (≈ 250 words → ~67 s at 3.7 words/s)

**HOOK**
1. In 1983, a brand-new jet ran out of fuel at forty-one thousand feet. Because of a maths mistake. *(F1, F5)*

**SETTING**
2. Canada was going metric. Air Canada's new Boeing thought in kilograms. *(F2)*
3. Its fuel gauges were broken, so the crew measured the tanks with a stick and did the sum by hand. *(F3)*

**ACTION — escalation**
4. They needed twenty-two thousand three hundred kilograms. They loaded twenty-two thousand three hundred… pounds. Less than half. *(F4)*
5. Over Ontario, an alarm beeped. Then another. Then both engines went quiet. *(F5)*
6. Then some of the screens went dark. *(F6)*

**SNAP**
7. Sixty-nine people were now in a very large glider. *(F7)*

**DEADPAN DOUBLE**
8. Luckily, the captain flew gliders for fun. *(F8)*
9. Unluckily, every big airport was too far. *(F9)*
10. But the co-pilot remembered an old air force base where he used to work. Gimli. *(F9)*

**EXPLANATION — one idea, everyday analogy**
11. A jet with no engines doesn't drop like a rock. It slides down the air, like a paper plane. *(F10)*
12. For every metre it sank, it slid twelve metres forward. Just enough. *(F10)*

**ESCALATION 2 — landing**
13. But the base was closed. It was a race track now. And today, there was a race. *(F11)*
14. Too high and too fast, the captain flew sideways, an old glider trick to dump height. *(F12)*
15. The nose wheel never locked. So the nose scraped down the runway… and worked as a brake. *(F13)*
16. Racers ran over with fire extinguishers. *(F14)*
17. Nobody died. *(F15)*

**CONSEQUENCE**
18. Investigators blamed the airline. Other crews tried it in a simulator. They crashed. *(F16, F17)*

**CALLBACK TWIST**
19. The jet flew twenty-five more years. Then it was cut up into luggage tags. *(F18)*
20. Weight limit: twenty-three kilograms. Or fifty pounds. Please check which. *(F18, A)*
